## Theorems about possibility sets
1. $A$ know $E$ has occured iff a triusm that implies $E$ has occured
2. $P\{\omega\}$ is the smallest truism containing $\omega$
3. $A$ know that $E$ has occured in state $\omega$ iff $P\{\omega\}\subseteq E$
